## Compliance Violations (Legal Risk)

### GDPR Violations (France/La Réunion)

1. **No Privacy Policy** - GDPR Article 13-14 violation
   - Required: Disclosure of data collection and processing
   - Penalty: Up to €20M or 4% of annual revenue

2. **No Cookie Consent** - GDPR Article 7-8 violation
   - Required: Explicit consent for cookies
   - Penalty: Up to €20M or 4% of annual revenue

3. **No Data Processing Disclosure** - GDPR Article 13 violation
   - Required: Information about data processing purposes
   - Penalty: Up to €10M or 2% of annual revenue

4. **No User Rights Information** - GDPR Article 15-22 violation
   - Required: Information about user rights (access, rectification, erasure)
   - Penalty: Up to €10M or 2% of annual revenue

**Note:** As a site based in La Réunion (France), GDPR applies. CNIL (French data protection authority) can impose fines.
